# Privacy Policy — Quikkk

**Effective date:** October 12, 2026
**Operator:** Mekyek Global Services Pvt Ltd ("we", "us", "our")
**Contact:** info@quikkk.com | +91 83359 74309 | https://quikkk.com

Quikkk is a loan and credit-card aggregation platform. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and your rights. By using the Quikkk app or website, you agree to this policy.

## 1. Data we collect

**Account data:** name, mobile number, email address — collected at sign-up via OTP verification.

**KYC & application data (only when you apply):** date of birth, PAN, address, employment and income details, bank statements — required by lending partners to assess eligibility. We collect this only with your explicit consent per application.

**Device & usage data:** device model, OS version, app version, crash logs, and anonymised usage analytics to improve the app.

**Permissions we may request (all optional, requested in-context):**
- Camera — only to scan documents you choose to upload for KYC
- Contacts — never accessed
- Location — never accessed
- SMS — never read; OTPs are auto-filled by the OS where supported

## 2. How we use your data

- To create and secure your account (OTP login)
- To match you with loan/credit-card offers from lending partners
- To process and track your applications
- To send transactional messages (OTP, application status, EMI reminders)
- To prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations
- We **never sell** your personal data.

## 3. Data sharing

We share data only as needed to provide the service:
- **Lending partners (banks/NBFCs):** only the application details you submit, and only after you choose to apply
- **Service providers:** SMS/email delivery (OTP, notifications), payment processing (UPI), cloud hosting — all bound by data-processing agreements
- **Legal:** when required by law, regulation, or court order

## 4. Data security

- OTP-based login (no stored passwords)
- TLS encryption in transit; encrypted storage at rest
- Role-based access control; audit logging of data access
- Session tokens expire automatically

## 5. Data retention

- Account data: retained while your account is active
- Application data: retained as required by financial regulations (typically 8 years for lending records under Indian law), then anonymised or deleted
- You may request deletion of your account at info@quikkk.com; regulatory records that must be retained will be anonymised where possible

## 6. Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data, and withdraw consent for marketing communications, by writing to info@quikkk.com. We respond within 30 days.

## 7. Children

Quikkk is for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We will notify you of material changes via the app or email. Continued use after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.

## 9. Grievance redressal

Grievance Officer: Mekyek Global Services Pvt Ltd, info@quikkk.com, +91 83359 74309. We acknowledge complaints within 24 hours and resolve them within 30 days, in line with applicable RBI digital-lending guidelines.
